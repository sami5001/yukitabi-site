Using Yukitabi

Travel Information

Yukitabi is an informational travel guide. We work to keep the content useful and accurate, but travel conditions can change. Transport services, prices, opening hours, entry rules, cultural guidance, and local procedures may vary by time, place, and provider.

Always verify time-sensitive details with official sources, transport operators, venues, hotels, airlines, airports, or local authorities before making decisions.

Emergency and Safety Information

Yukitabi is not an emergency service, legal service, medical service, or substitute for professional advice. In urgent situations, contact local emergency services, nearby staff, your accommodation, your embassy or consulate, or another appropriate authority.

The app is designed around bundled content and local device storage. Local progress, favorites, preferences, and trip details may be lost if you delete the app, erase device data, or switch devices without a device backup.
