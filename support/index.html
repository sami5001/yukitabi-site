Common Questions

Does Yukitabi work offline?

Yes. Core travel guides, checklists, phrases, airport guidance, and cultural tips are bundled with the app.

Does Yukitabi collect personal data?

No personal data is collected by the iOS app. Favorites, checklist progress, trip details, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Is Yukitabi free?

Current pricing is shown in your regional App Store. Yukitabi has no account requirement or ads. The core guide works offline; optional Siri and Apple Intelligence features depend on your device, language, region and system availability.

Can I suggest a correction or new guide?

Yes. Email the suggestion and include the relevant city, airport, dish, phrase, or guide title if possible.

How do I open my dietary card quickly?

In version 1.1, save your selections in Phrases → My Allergy Card. Add Yukitabi’s Dietary Card widget when customising your Lock Screen, or choose Dietary Card from Control Center’s control gallery on iOS 18 or later. Tap to open the large Japanese card; if no card is saved, setup opens instead.

The widget and control use a generic label and icon. Your allergy and dietary selections stay inside the app. Unlocking or authentication may be required.

Why did my checklist or favorites disappear?

Yukitabi stores this data locally. If you delete the app or reset your device data, local progress is removed too.

Travel and Safety Notes

Yukitabi is an informational travel companion. Transport schedules, entry procedures, business hours, prices, and local rules can change, so verify time-sensitive details with official providers before relying on them.

In an emergency, contact local emergency services or nearby staff immediately. Do not rely on any travel app as your only source of emergency help.
