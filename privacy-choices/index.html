Privacy Choices

No account. No server-side app profile.

Yukitabi's iOS app does not collect personal data, so there is no hosted app account, profile, or analytics record to access, export, or delete.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Your Choices in the App

Clear local app data
Use available in-app reset controls where shown. To remove all local Yukitabi data from your device, delete the app from iOS.
Stop app tracking
No action is needed. Yukitabi does not track you across apps or websites.
Manage permissions
Core travel help needs no location, contacts, microphone or notification permission. Optional menu text recognition uses the system photo picker for images you select; it does not request general access to your photo library. Siri and Apple Intelligence are controlled in iOS Settings.

Website and Email Choices

If you emailed support, you can ask us to remove your message or email address by contacting hello@yukitabi.app.
