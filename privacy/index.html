Privacy Policy

The Yukitabi app does not collect personal data.

Yukitabi is an offline-first iPhone and iPad app. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, and no backend service in version 1.1.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

App Privacy Summary

Accounts

No account, profile, or registration is required.

Tracking

No tracking, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking are used.

Analytics

No analytics SDKs or usage analytics are included in the app.

Network

Core app content is bundled and designed to work offline.

App Store Privacy Label

Apple's App Privacy label focuses on data collected from the app, including whether data is linked to you or used to track you. For Yukitabi version 1.1, the App Store Connect response is: No, we do not collect data from this app.

Data Not Collected

The Yukitabi iOS app does not transmit personal data, identifiers, diagnostics, location, or usage data to Yukitabi or third-party partners.

App Store Connect answer

Data Used to Track You

None. Yukitabi does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

Data Linked to You

None. The app has no account system and collects no app data linked to identity.

Data Not Linked to You

None. The app does not collect anonymized or de-identified usage data either.

On-device only: favorites, checklist progress, trip details, and display preferences are stored locally on your device. Apple explains that data processed only on device is not considered collected for App Privacy answers unless it is sent off device.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Yukitabi stores a small amount of app state locally so the app can remember your preferences and progress. This may include:

  • Fieldbook pages, pinned entries and private travel notes
  • Allergy and dietary card selections
  • Checklist completion status
  • Favorites for guides, phrases, dishes, and tips
  • Trip name and trip date, if you choose to enter them
  • Display preferences, such as language display and appearance settings

This information is stored on your device using standard iOS storage. It is not sent to Yukitabi, sold, shared, or used for tracking.

Data Collected by the iOS App

The iOS app collects no personal data. It does not collect location, contacts, photos, health data, financial data, identifiers, diagnostics, or usage data.

The app declares use of iOS local storage APIs because it stores preferences and progress on your device.

Website and Support Data

The public website is separate from the iOS app. The website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tracking.

If you email hello@yukitabi.app, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to support requests, feedback, legal questions, or content corrections.

Apple system features and optional assistance

Yukitabi indexes bundled public guide content for Spotlight and Siri. Saved Fieldbook page names are supplied to Apple's App Shortcuts system so you can open a page by name. Private Fieldbook notes and personal allergy selections are not indexed. Apple processes Siri requests under its own Siri and Apple Intelligence settings and policies; that system service may require connectivity.

Optional Fieldbook matching uses an on-device language model and reviewed entry descriptions. A photo you select through Apple's photo picker is processed for text on device and is not uploaded to Yukitabi. Private Fieldbook notes are excluded from model matching. If your selected image is stored in iCloud, Apple's picker may download it before local processing.

You can use manual search and the bundled guide without enabling these optional features.

Third-Party Services

Yukitabi version 1.1 does not include third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs in the iOS app.

Outside the app, we may rely on standard service providers to operate email support and app distribution through Apple. These providers process only the information needed for those services.

Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you clear it through available app controls, delete the app, or erase device data.

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain reasonable support records.

See Privacy Choices for practical ways to clear local app data or request removal of website/support data.

Children's Privacy

Yukitabi is a general travel guide app and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If Yukitabi's data practices change, we will update this page and the date above. App Store privacy answers will also be updated when required.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@yukitabi.app.
